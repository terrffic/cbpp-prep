先读这段：五套卷不是五次独立测评READ FIRST — THE FIVE PAPERS ARE NOT INDEPENDENT

五套卷共用同一 — 题题库，两两之间重合 —，五套合计只覆盖 — 道不同题目。所以不要把五次成绩当作五次独立预测：建议只用第 1 套与第 3 套做阶段性测评（两者重合 —），其余三套作为强化练习。分章练习的题池就是本域题库（每章 30–41 题），因此练习与模考会遇到原题——这是设计使然，练习用于掌握，模考用于检验。

All five papers draw on the same —-item bank; any two papers overlap by —, and the five together cover only — distinct items. Treat them as practice sets, not five independent measurements: use Paper 1 and Paper 3 as milestone checks (they overlap by —) and the rest for reinforcement. Chapter drills draw on the same per-domain bank (30–41 items each), so items can reappear between drills and mocks by design.
